# Customer Analytics & Revenue Forecasting Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **customer subscription data** using data analytics and data science techniques to uncover meaningful insights related to customer behavior, revenue performance, subscription patterns, and customer retention.

The analysis helps understand key **revenue drivers**, identify customer trends, evaluate subscription performance, and support data-driven business decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA)
* Clean and preprocess customer subscription data
* Analyze customer behavior and subscription patterns
* Identify key factors influencing total revenue
* Analyze customer tenure and its relationship with revenue
* Identify revenue trends and customer segments
* Perform revenue forecasting to understand future trends
* Generate actionable business insights

## 📈 Key Insights

* **Customer tenure has a strong relationship with total revenue.**
* Longer-tenure customers contribute significantly to overall revenue.
* Customer subscription characteristics influence revenue performance.
* Customer segments show different revenue and behavioral patterns.
* Revenue trends can be analyzed to support future business planning.
* Forecasting provides an estimate of future revenue trends.
